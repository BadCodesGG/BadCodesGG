#### Out in the open

**[Questy](https://github.com/BadCodesGG/Questy)** reads Escape from Tarkov's logs and tracks
PvE quest progress on an interactive map. Electron, entirely local, no account, no telemetry.

**[Sim Garage](https://github.com/BadCodesGG/Sim-Garage)** finds every racing sim you have
installed, sorts out their configs, and launches them from one place. Auto-updating desktop app.

**[sandbox-fivem](https://github.com/BadCodesGG/sandbox-fivem)** is an archived FiveM framework:
React interfaces, Lua gameplay resources, MongoDB and MariaDB behind it. Forked 81 times, which
still surprises me.

**[fivem-fake-browser](https://github.com/BadCodesGG/fivem-fake-browser)** is an in-game browser
resource from 2023. Archived, and still the thing most people know me for.

#### Behind the curtain

[Daniel Newman Racing](https://www.danielnewmanracing.com) is a commercial sim-racing platform
with around 7,000 racers on it: subscriptions and billing, authentication, live telemetry over
WebSockets, and hardware integrations for race-day control.

The day job is a React and TypeScript platform, plus the tooling the engineering team runs on:
PHP, Python, Docker, nginx. Six years with the same team through a rename and an acquisition,
three of those leading front-end.
